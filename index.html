About V. K. Dubey Associates: Justice for All

EST. 2011

Upholding access to justice, professional integrity, and courtroom excellence across India.

Established in 2011 by Advocate Vinay Kumar Dubey, V. K. Dubey Associates is a Mumbai-Delhi based law firm committed to providing comprehensive, strategic and result-oriented legal services. Built on the principles of professional integrity, legal excellence and access to justice, the firm has developed its practice across diverse areas of law, representing individuals, businesses, corporate entities, institutions and other stakeholders.

With a strong foundation in litigation and legal advisory, the firm offers representation and legal assistance in Civil Litigation, Criminal Litigation, Family Law, Banking and Corporate Matters, and proceedings under the Negotiable Instruments Act, among other areas. The firm regularly handles complex legal disputes and represents clients before the Bombay High Court, Supreme Court of India, District and Trial Courts, Tribunals and other appropriate judicial and quasi-judicial forums across India.

Under the leadership of its founder, Advocate Vinay Kumar Dubey, the firm emphasises meticulous legal research, precise drafting, strategic case preparation and effective courtroom advocacy. Its approach is centred on understanding the legal and practical dimensions of every matter and developing appropriate strategies to protect clients' rights and interests. Over the years, V. K. Dubey Associates has remained committed to delivering accessible, ethical and professional legal services, while upholding the belief that justice should be available to all, irrespective of background or circumstance.

At V. K. Dubey Associates, it is believed that every legal challenge deserves a thoughtful strategy, dedicated representation and an unwavering commitment to justice.

Legal Practice & Institutional Roles

Advocate Vinay Kumar Dubey (V. K. Dubey) is a Mumbai- and Delhi-based legal professional known for his expertise in Civil, Criminal, Banking and Corporate matters. He primarily practices before the Bombay High Court and various trial courts and is also actively involved in matters before the Supreme Court of India, DRT and NCLT, including high-profile cases concerning the banking and industrial sectors. He is the Chief of the Mumbai & Delhi-based law firm V. K. Dubey Associates, which operates under the motto “Justice for All.” He is also associated with United Legal, a law firm dedicated to providing legal services in banking, corporate and settlement matters, with over a decade of experience in the legal field. In addition to his legal practice, Mr. Dubey serves as a Member of the Hindi Advisory Committee under the Government of India.

Social Welfare & Philanthropy

Beyond his professional commitments, he has established several organisations, including the Bharat International Business Organisation (BIBO) Foundation, Brahman Ekta Manch Charitable Trust and Gyanodaya Seva Samiti, through which he actively contributes to social welfare initiatives. He also serves as the National Legal Head of Prime Minister’s Atmanirbhar Bharat Abhiyan Sanghthan and is associated with grassroots social and community initiatives. His philanthropic activities include supporting underprivileged communities, promoting education, providing assistance to the economically weaker sections of society and working towards the protection and welfare of cattle. Through various charitable initiatives, including Kumari Mamta Devi Charitable Trust and Gyanodaya Seva Samiti, he continues to contribute towards social empowerment, particularly in Pratapgarh, Uttar Pradesh, while maintaining his professional base in Mumbai.

Honours, Recognition & Philosophy

In recognition of his contributions to the legal profession and society, Mr. Dubey has received several accolades in 2024 and 2026, including the “Popular Civilian Icon of India Award.” Advocate V. K. Dubey was also honoured at the 2nd International Bar & Bench Badminton Championship held in London, recognising his contribution and association with the legal fraternity. His work reflects a continued commitment to legal service, social responsibility and community development. Through V. K. Dubey Associates, he continues to advance a client-focused approach to legal services, combining professional expertise with dedication to the principles of justice and fairness.

Practice Area

Civil Litigation

Our Civil Litigation practice provides comprehensive legal representation and strategic advice across a broad spectrum of civil and commercial disputes. We assist individuals, businesses, companies, developers, institutions and other clients in protecting and enforcing their legal and contractual rights. Our approach combines detailed legal research, meticulous case preparation, strategic advice and effective representation at every stage of litigation. We advise and represent clients in disputes involving contracts, property, real estate, commercial transactions, recovery of money, possession, ownership, injunctions, specific performance, declarations, construction and development matters, partnership disputes, succession and inheritance, and other civil claims. Our lawyers work closely with clients to understand the factual and commercial dimensions of each dispute and formulate a litigation strategy aligned with their objectives. Our practice encompasses the complete lifecycle of civil proceedings, beginning with pre-litigation assessment and legal notices and extending through pleadings, interim applications, evidence, examination and cross-examination, arguments, judgment, appeals, revisions and execution proceedings. We also assist clients in evaluating settlement opportunities, negotiation and alternative dispute-resolution mechanisms where appropriate.

We have experience in handling matters concerning breach of contractual obligations, recovery proceedings, property and title disputes, landlord-tenant disputes, possession and eviction matters, injunction proceedings, specific performance of contracts, construction and infrastructure disputes, developer and society disputes, partnership and business disputes, and succession-related claims. Our team also advises clients on preventive and strategic aspects of civil litigation, including contractual risk assessment, documentation, legal notices, preservation of evidence, limitation issues, jurisdictional considerations and available civil remedies. We focus on identifying potential legal risks at an early stage and developing appropriate strategies to protect our clients’ interests.

Where proceedings are initiated, our lawyers undertake comprehensive preparation of pleadings and supporting documents, formulate appropriate interim and final reliefs, and represent clients before the appropriate courts and forums. We place particular emphasis on precision in drafting, substantive legal research, procedural compliance and focused courtroom advocacy. Through a combination of strategic legal advice, rigorous preparation and effective representation, V. K. Dubey Associates assists clients in navigating complex civil disputes while seeking practical and legally sustainable solutions.

Criminal Litigation

Our Criminal Litigation practice provides comprehensive legal representation and strategic advice across a wide range of criminal matters. We represent individuals, businesses, corporate entities, accused persons, complainants and other stakeholders at various stages of criminal proceedings, with a focus on protecting legal rights and developing effective legal strategies. Our practice covers FIR-related matters, criminal complaints, investigations, bail and anticipatory bail, discharge applications, criminal trials, appeals, revisions and proceedings before the High Courts, Supreme Court and other appropriate courts and forums across India. We handle matters involving economic and financial offences, fraud, cheating, criminal breach of trust, forgery, property-related offences, offences against persons, cyber offences and matrimonial or family-related criminal proceedings. Our team assists clients in matters concerning police investigations, notices, arrest, search and seizure, complaints before Magistrates and interim reliefs, as well as criminal proceedings arising from commercial and corporate transactions.

We also undertake trial advocacy, drafting of pleadings and applications, examination and cross-examination of witnesses, analysis of documentary and electronic evidence, appeals and revisions. Our approach combines detailed legal research, careful preparation and focused courtroom representation while maintaining confidentiality and attention to procedural safeguards. Through strategic advice and effective representation, V. K. Dubey Associates assists clients in navigating complex criminal proceedings and pursuing appropriate remedies available under law.

Family Law

Our Family Law practice provides comprehensive legal advice, representation and dispute-resolution services in matters concerning marriage, matrimonial relationships, family rights and personal law. We assist individuals and families with a practical, confidential and sensitive approach to resolving complex family disputes. Our practice covers divorce, judicial separation, restitution of conjugal rights, maintenance, alimony, child custody, guardianship, adoption, domestic violence, succession and inheritance matters. We represent clients in both contested and mutual-consent divorce proceedings, from initial consultation and drafting through evidence, arguments and final adjudication. We also handle custody and visitation disputes, matrimonial cruelty, harassment, domestic violence and related civil and criminal proceedings, coordinating legal strategies where multiple proceedings arise from the same family dispute.

Our practice extends to succession, inheritance, wills, probate, letters of administration and family property disputes, as well as pre-litigation negotiations, mediation and settlement of matrimonial and family matters. Given the sensitive nature of family disputes, we place particular emphasis on confidentiality, careful communication and personalised legal advice. Where litigation becomes necessary, we represent clients before Family Courts, District Courts, High Courts, the Supreme Court and other appropriate forums across India. Through strategic advice, thorough preparation and effective representation, V. K. Dubey Associates assists clients in navigating family-law matters and pursuing appropriate legal remedies.

Practice Area

Negotiable Instruments Act Matters

Our practice provides legal advice and representation in matters arising under the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881, with particular focus on cheque dishonour and related financial disputes. We represent individuals, businesses, companies, creditors and other stakeholders in proceedings concerning payment defaults and negotiable instruments. We handle matters involving dishonour of cheques under Section 138, issuance of statutory legal notices, complaints, defence in cheque dishonour proceedings, recovery of dues and related civil and criminal proceedings. Our team assists clients from the stage of legal notice and pre-litigation negotiations through filing of complaints, interim proceedings, evidence, trial, settlement, compounding, appeals and revisions, as applicable. We also advise clients on documentation, limitation, liability, evidentiary requirements and procedural aspects associated with cheque dishonour matters and represent them before the appropriate courts and forums.
